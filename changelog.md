## 2.0.0

Shulker Palette is rebuilt for Minecraft 26.3.

### Added

- Three new modes alongside random by count: equal random, pick, and gradient.
- A column of buttons on the shulker box screen to turn the palette on or off, switch modes, and save or load the palette.
- The palette modifier key, Left Alt by default and rebindable. Holding it lets you scroll through picks and gradient rows, place the shulker box itself, or open the shulker box in your hand.
- The next block shows inside the box in your hotbar and in your hand, and a view of the box appears above the hotbar while you scroll.
- Repaint, in creative, which replaces the block you're looking at with the palette's next block.
- Save and Load through the clipboard, as text you can edit. Loading in survival adds a cheat sheet that shows which blocks go in each slot.
- A palette box keeps its palette when it's broken.
- Toggle palette and Toggle repaint keys, both unbound by default.

### Changed

- Requires MenuKit 7.2.0 or newer and SlotKit 1.1.0 or newer.
- Needed on both the client and the server.
- Palettes from 1.0.0 don't carry over. The blocks inside stay where they are, and one click on the box's power button, or the Toggle palette key, makes it a palette again.
- The Inventory Plus peek toggle is removed, since Inventory Plus no longer has a peek view.
